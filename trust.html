DATA & TRUST

How Blockwise uses data

Blockwise reads the NYC Department of Housing Preservation and Development’s public Housing Maintenance Code Violations dataset. We do not file reports, edit city records, or send information to landlords or agencies.

What the results mean

Results are a research aid, not a safety rating, legal advice, or a statement about a building’s current condition. City records may be incomplete, delayed, or associated with an address differently than a listing.

Privacy

Blockwise does not require an account. Searches are used only to retrieve public records and are rate-limited to protect the service. We do not sell personal information.

Source and corrections

Data comes from NYC Open Data. Maps include OpenStreetMap contributors data. For corrections to a government record, contact the City agency that maintains it.
